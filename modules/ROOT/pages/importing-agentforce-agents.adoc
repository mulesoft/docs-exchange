= Importing Agentforce Agents
ifndef::env-site,env-github[]
include::_attributes.adoc[]
endif::[]
:imagesdir: ../assets/images/

Organizations can import their Agentforce agents into Exchange to include them in business groups.

Importing from the same Salesforce organization into a different business group can create duplicate assets in Exchange.

Imported Agentforce agents appear in *Agents & Tools*.

. Select *Settings* from the navigation bar and select the *Asset import* tab.
. To change the Salesforce org, xref:access-management::managing-connected-salesforce-orgs.adoc[enable the connection in Access Management].
. From *Settings* in Exchange, click *Import* and click *Confirm*.
+
Agentagents are imported in the current business group.
. Close the dialog and refresh the page after a few minutes.
. Verify that the Agentforce agents are listed in the Exchange catalog on the *Agents & Tools* page.
