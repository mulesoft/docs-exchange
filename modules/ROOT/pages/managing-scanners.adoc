[[run-a-scanner]]
== Run a Scanner
Run the scanner manually to capture new assets and add them to Exchange. 
Scanners run once daily and retrieve up to 1,200 assets per scan, with a maximum runtime of 6 hours. Exchange retains scan logs and statuses for at least 90 days and never deletes the most recent run. Run one scan at a time for each provider.

. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Find the scanner in the list, click the more options icon (image:more-options-icon.png[2%,2%]) and then select *Run Now*.
+
The scan waits in the queue until the current scan finishes.

[[view-scanner-details]]
== View Scanner Details
View a list of scanners and the details about their scans. The *Scanners* page lists the scanners added for the business group. The scanner details show information about the scanner, connection, last completed run, and scan history.

Scanner statuses are:

* Scheduled
* Paused
* Pending review
* Queued
* Running
* Importing
* Stopped

To see more information about a scanner and its scan details, from the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners* and click the name of a scanner from the list.

Here's an example details page:

The *Actions* menu on the scanner details page includes these options, depending on the scanner status:

|===
|*Scanner Status* |*Actions Menu Options*
|Scheduled or Paused |Run Now

|Stopped |Edit Scanner

|===

[[review-scan-results-manually]]
== Review Scan Results Manually
Review the scan results and determine which assets to import into Exchange. When a scanner's scheduled run configuration is set to *Ask to review*, its status changes to *Pending review* in the scanner details page after the scan completes. Otherwise, the scan results are automatically imported into Exchange if set to *Auto-resolve*.

While reviewing scan results, note these behaviors:

* If another scan completes while you are reviewing, the current queue becomes stale and can’t be submitted. It remains available read‑only in the audit history. Open the newest queue to continue. If another scan configuration already registered the same asset, the system skips or updates as needed (no downgrades) and logs the outcome.

* If you don't confirm the scan result changes, the assets are not imported into Exchange. The scan results are available in the audit log. When the scanner runs again, the assets are scanned again and the previous results are stale and are overwritten by the new scan results. Consider updating the scanner configuration to *Auto-resolve* to automatically import assets without manual  review.

Here's what you see in the scanner's list page:

image:scanners-list.png["Scanners list page"]

* A tabular list of discovered assets that respects the filters in the scanner configuration.
* Key columns: Scanner Status, Scanner Name, Provider, Service Type, Last Completed Run, Last Run Status, Next Run, Total Services

For each scanner in the list, you can perform these actions from the more options menu (image:more-options-icon.png[2%,2%]):

* Run the scanner now
* Edit the scanner configuration
* Delete the scanner

Select the scanner with a status of *Pending review* to open the review details and click *Review & Import*.

image:scan-details-for-manual-review.png["Scan details for manual review"]

These tabs indicate how each service will be handled:

* Add: first discovery; not yet in the registry
* Update: Already in the registry; metadata changed at source
* Missing: In the registry but not found in this scan; if in use, show “missing from source”; if not used, it can be removed after a retention window (for example, 30 days)
* Existing: Already in the Exchange; no changes detected
* Skipped: Services that were deselected in a prior review

Select what to import:

* All asset are selected by default. Deselect individual assets, or deselect all and pick only the ones to import.
* Deselected assets aren’t imported now and aren’t added to the ignore list; they remain pending and will reappear in the next scan. Previously deselected items show a “Previously Skipped” badge; if the source version changed, the badge indicates a newer version is available.
Skip assets from future scans

//* Use the action menu to add an asset to the Skipped (ignore) list. Skipped services won’t appear in future scan results for that source unless you remove them from the Skipped list.
* The Skipped list view lets you review and restore ignored assets.

Queue freshness and rescans:

* If another scan completes while you’re reviewing, the current review queue becomes stale and can’t be submitted; it’s preserved read‑only in audit history, and a banner directs you to the newest queue.
* A “Last scanned X days ago” label indicates freshness. If available, use Rescan to refresh results without losing your current selections.

Multiple scan configurations:

* If another scan configuration already registered a service:

  ** Same version: Skip and log
  ** Newer version: Update in place and log the version change
  ** Older version: Skip (no downgrade) and log
  ** Flagged for deletion but already removed: treat as success and log
* After import, a summary shows what was imported, updated, or skipped and why.

To manually review the scan results:

. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Click the scanner with a status of *Pending review* to open the review details.
. Review the list of discovered assets. The table shows:
+
* Provider/Source (for example, AWS Bedrock, Google Vertex AI, Microsoft Copilot)
* Assets name
* Assets ID (source identifier)
* Status (Active/Inactive from the source)
* Last updated (from the source)
* Foundational model (when available)
. Choose what to import:
+
.. Keep assets selected to include them.
.. Deselect any assets you don’t want to import now (the Import button shows the selection count, for example, “Import 4 of 9 assets”).
. Optionally skip assets from future scans:
+
.. For an asset, open the action menu and select *Skip* to add it to the Skipped list.
.. Skipped assets don’t appear in future scan results for this source until you remove them from the Skipped list.
. Click *Review changes* and confirm the summary:
+
* Imported: New assets to add
* Updated: Existing assets whose records will be refreshed
* Skipped: Assets added to the Skipped list
. Click *Import* to apply changes.

[[abort-scans]]
== Abort Scans
Abort scans for a scanner when its status is queued, running, or importing. When the scan is aborted, the status is updated to stopped.

* To abort a scan from the *Scanners* page:
+
. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Find the scanner in the list, click the more options icon (image:more-options-icon.png[2%,2%]) and then select *Abort Scan*.

* To abort a scan from the scanner details page, click *Actions* and then select *Abort Scan*. 

[[delete-a-scanner]]
== Delete a Scanner
Delete an unused scanner when its status is scheduled, paused or stopped. Choose whether to keep or delete the assets associated with the scanner. Imported assets remain in Exchange if instances exist in API Manager.

. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Find the scanner in the list, click the more options icon (image:more-options-icon.png[2%,2%]) and then select *Delete Scanner*.
. Choose whether to keep or delete imported assets and click *Delete*.

[[edit-scanner-configuration]]
== Edit Scanner Configuration
Edit settings for a scanner with a status of scheduled, paused, or stopped, and turn scheduled scans on or off.

* To edit a scanner from the *Scanners* page:
+
. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Find the scanner in the list, click the more options icon (image:more-options-icon.png[2%,2%]) and then select *Edit Scanner*.
. Make your changes, and click *Update*.

* To edit a scanner from the scanner details page:
+
. Click *Actions* and then select *Edit Scanner*.
. Make your changes, and click *Update*.

[[view-audit-logs]]
== View Audit Logs
View audit logs for scanners. In the audit log, view scan details, such as assets that were imported, updated, removed, and already in Exchange, and unsuccessful scans. The available tabs depend on the scan results.

Here's an example audit log:

image:scanner-audit-log.png["Audit log showing details of a scan event"]

. From the sidebar in Exchange, click *Scanners*.
. Select the scanner from the list.
. Select an event from *Scan History*. 
. To view imported assets, click the *Imported* tab.
. To view updated assets with newer versions available, click the *Updated* tab.
. To view assets removed from Exchange because they’re missing from the provider, click the *Removed* tab.
. To view assets that are found in the provider’s system and already in Exchange, click the *Already in Exchange* tab.
. To view unsuccessful scans, click the *Unsuccessful* tab.
